What’s inside

Everything you need to run the lot

Inventory Management

Add units by VIN, track costs and reconditioning, and see days-on-lot at a glance.

Deal Desking

Build cash, finance and buy-here-pay-here deals with taxes, fees and trade-ins calculated for you.

Command Center modules

Find your money — and find it fast.

⚠

Dead Money Command Center

See every dollar that’s stuck. Dead Money Command Center shows you the aged units, the money you’ve sunk into them, and what they’re costing you every day they sit, so you know what to move first.

  • Aged inventory ranked by dollars tied up
  • Total cost per unit, including reconditioning
  • Spot units that need a price change or a trip to auction
💰

Cash Command Center

Find your money and find it fast. Cash Command Center puts all your dealership’s cash on one screen: what’s in the bank, what’s tied up in inventory, and what’s still owed to you.

  • Cash on hand vs. cash tied up in the lot
  • Money owed to you: deals not yet funded, payments due
  • One screen, updated as deals happen
