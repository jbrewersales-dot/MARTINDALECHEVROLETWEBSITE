About Martindale Chevrolet

Martindale Chevrolet is a locally owned dealership in New Madrid, Missouri, serving families and businesses across the Bootheel. We sell new Chevrolet vehicles and quality used vehicles of every make, and our service department keeps them on the road.

We believe in straight talk, fair prices, and treating every customer like a neighbor — because they are.
