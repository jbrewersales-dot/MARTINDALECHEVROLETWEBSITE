Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how K2B Network Solutions LLC (“K2B,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit k2bnetworksolutions.com, use our software, contact us, or receive text messages (SMS/MMS) or phone calls from any phone number owned or operated by or on behalf of K2B Network Solutions LLC.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us. Your name, business name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and anything you include in a message, demo request, or support request.
  • Text message and call information. Your mobile number, your consent to receive messages, the content of messages you send us, your opt-in and opt-out history, and call details such as the date, time, length of a call, and the phone numbers involved.
  • Customer account information. If your business uses our software, the information needed to set up and support your account.
  • Website information. Basic technical information your browser sends automatically, such as your device type, browser type, and pages visited.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your questions and demo requests;
  • Provide, operate, support, and improve our software and services;
  • Send you the text messages and make the phone calls described below that you agreed to receive;
  • Send account, billing, and service notices;
  • Keep records of your consent and opt-out requests; and
  • Follow the law and protect our rights, our customers, and the public.

3. Text messaging (SMS) program

Your mobile information is never sold or shared for marketing.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories of information sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. This information will not be shared with any third parties, except with service providers that help us deliver our messages (such as our text messaging platform and wireless carriers), and only so they can deliver those messages.

What messages you may receive

If you opt in, K2B Network Solutions LLC may send you text messages about:

  • Demo scheduling, appointment confirmations, and reminders;
  • Replies to your questions and customer support conversations;
  • Account, service, and billing notices for our software; and
  • Product updates or offers from K2B, but only if you specifically agreed to receive marketing messages.

How you opt in

You opt in by giving us your mobile number and agreeing to receive text messages. You might do this by checking a consent box on our website form, texting us first, or telling us verbally or in writing. Agreeing to receive text messages is not a condition of buying any product or service.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile phone plan.

How to opt out

You can stop receiving text messages at any time. Reply STOP to any message (you can also reply END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, or QUIT). We will send one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and you will not receive any more text messages from us unless you opt in again. To rejoin, reply START.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or contact us at (573) 620-5630 or jbrewersales@gmail.com.

Carrier disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers may change at any time.

4. Phone calls

  • We call you only when you have contacted us, asked us to call, are an existing customer, or otherwise gave us permission to call.
  • We do not make sales calls to numbers on the National Do Not Call Registry unless you have given us permission or have an existing business relationship with us, as allowed by law.
  • Calls with K2B may be recorded or monitored for quality, training, and record-keeping purposes. Where the law requires it, we will tell you at the start of the call that it is being recorded.
  • You can ask us to stop calling you at any time by telling us during a call, or by contacting us using the information below. We will add your number to our internal do-not-call list and honor your request within a reasonable time, as required by law.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it:

  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as hosting, email, phone, and text messaging providers. They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • When required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of K2B, our customers, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of our company, where the information would remain covered by this policy.

As stated in Section 3, text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

6. Data security & retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending or storing information is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, or as required by law. We keep records of text message consent and opt-outs as long as needed to prove compliance.

7. Your choices & rights

  • Text messages: reply STOP at any time.
  • Phone calls: ask us to stop calling and we will add you to our do-not-call list.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contact us.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: contact us to ask what information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We will respond as required by applicable law.

8. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
